01 — THE IDEAABOUT MIYO

Make it
yours.

MIYO is a small creative project about the details that make each of us unmistakably ourselves—and the connections that let us share them.

Made in the spirit of a student exchange between Trường Ngôi Sao Hoàng Mai in Hanoi and Trường Trung Sơn in Kaohsiung, MIYO creates room for curiosity, conversation, and memories made together.

What is MIYO? +

MIYO means “Make It Yours.” It is a creative project inspired by the student exchange between Trường Ngôi Sao Hoàng Mai in Hanoi and Trường Trung Sơn in Kaohsiung.

Who is the project for? +

It is for the students and educators taking part in this exchange.
