Made in the spirit of a student exchange between Hoang Mai Star School in Hanoi and Chung Shan Industrial & Commercial School in Kaohsiung, MIYO creates room for curiosity, conversation, and memories made together.

04 — GOOD TO KNOWFAQ

A few
answers.

What is MIYO? +

MIYO means “Make It Yours.” It is a creative project inspired by the student exchange between Hoang Mai Star School in Hanoi and Chung Shan Industrial & Commercial School in Kaohsiung.

Who is the project for? +

It is for the students and educators taking part in this exchange. The Questions page is intended for participants who have received an access code from their teacher.

What happens on the Questions page? +

Participants can share a few personal details, interests, preferences, and a photo. The form explains its photo consent request before submission. Please use the page only if you have been invited to participate.

When and where will activities take place? +

The public project details do not currently include confirmed dates or an activity schedule. Please check with your school for verified information.

Where can I ask a question? +

A public contact address has not been provided yet. Please contact your school or the teacher coordinating your exchange.

05 — TRANSPARENCYPRIVACY NOTES

DRAFT — REQUIRES SCHOOL REVIEW

Your details
deserve care.

This draft describes the questionnaire as currently implemented. It is not a substitute for confirmation by the schools responsible for the project.

What the form asks for

The Questions page uses an access code and may ask for a participant's name, nickname, role, class or position, zodiac sign, interests, favorite color, food preferences, and a photo. The form also records a photo-consent choice. Do not submit information unless you are an invited participant and understand the request.

Why information is requested

The form presents these questions to help the exchange team learn participant preferences and prepare for the exchange. The responsible schools must confirm the exact purposes and who may use each item before this draft is published as a final policy.

Storage, access, and photos

The questionnaire sends submissions to a Google Apps Script endpoint. The local backend source is written to append answers to Google Sheets and save uploaded photos to Google Drive, but the deployed configuration and current access settings have not been independently verified. The backend source describes uploaded files as private in Drive; the schools must confirm actual account access, sharing settings, storage region, safeguards, and any onward sharing.

Third-party assets

This page requests typefaces from Google Fonts. A visitor's browser connects to Google's font servers to load them. The project owners should review the provider's current data handling and confirm whether this external font service is appropriate before public release.

Editing, AI, and other processing

The questionnaire asks for consent to let the “Trung Sơn team” use and edit a photo for internal use. Whether images are processed with AI or any other external editing service has not been confirmed. No AI-processing practice is stated here; the project owners must confirm this before launch.

Retention, deletion, and consent

No retention period, deletion process, privacy contact, or process for withdrawing consent has been provided. These details—and any requirements for participants under 18 and their guardians—must be confirmed by both schools. To ask about an existing response, contact the teacher who gave you your access code.

PUBLISHING BLOCKER: confirm the outstanding privacy details with both schools before treating this draft as final.
